\section{Outline of the Dissertation}

\revise{The remainder of this dissertation is organized into four parts that follow
the lifecycle of high-stakes software. Rather than collecting all background in one
place, each technical chapter introduces the preliminaries and notation it needs.}

\revise{\noindent \textbf{Part I: Prologue.} Chapter~\ref{chapter:intro}, the present
chapter, motivates the thesis through three real incidents, reviews the most
relevant prior work, and states our contributions.}

\revise{\noindent \textbf{Part II: Securing High-Stakes Software Development}
addresses untrusted development. Chapter~2 presents our ongoing work on defending
coding agents against search- and documentation-level poisoning. Chapter~3 presents
\textsc{Scam2Prompt}, an audit of malicious scam endpoints in LLM-assisted code
generation~\cite{chen2025scam2prompt} (under submission).}

\revise{\noindent \textbf{Part III: Securing High-Stakes Software Deployment}
addresses untrusted use. Chapter~4 presents \textsc{CrossGuard}, control-flow
integrity for smart contracts~\cite{chen2025secure} (ICSE~2026). Chapter~5
presents \textsc{Trace2Inv}, runtime invariants for deployed smart
contracts~\cite{chen2024demystifying} (FSE~2024).}

\revise{\noindent \textbf{Part IV: Epilogue.} Chapter~6 concludes the dissertation
and discusses broader lessons and directions for future work.}
