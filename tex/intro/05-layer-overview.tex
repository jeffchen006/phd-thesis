\section{Security Layers Covered in This Thesis}

\noindent \textbf{Layer I: Execution-Semantic Foundations.}
We establish precise EVM-grounded models for reasoning about transaction effects and feasibility.

\noindent \textbf{Layer II: Runtime and Interaction Integrity.}
We study enforcement strategies for runtime invariants and cross-contract control-flow consistency, emphasizing effectiveness and operational overhead.

\noindent \textbf{Layer III: AI-Assisted Development Risk.}
We formalize the risk of malicious artifacts in generated code under developer-style prompts and connect this risk to secure software lifecycle concerns.
