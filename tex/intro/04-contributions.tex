\section{Thesis Contributions}

This thesis makes the following contributions.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{A defense framing for high-stakes software systems in untrusted environments.}
    We formulate a common security perspective for systems whose behavior may be shaped by arbitrary user actions,
    adversarial interactions, opaque training data, or weakly trusted online information.

    \item \textbf{A unified formal interface.}
    We provide a consistent notation and modeling interface for states, traces,
    objectives, and policy constraints, enabling direct comparison across
    different technical components.

    \item \textbf{Deployment-oriented technical methods across multiple stages of operation.}
    We develop and analyze methods that either constrain high-stakes execution at runtime
    or reveal harmful behavior induced by untrusted information sources,
    while accounting for practical constraints such as gas overhead and realistic developer workflows.
\end{itemize}
