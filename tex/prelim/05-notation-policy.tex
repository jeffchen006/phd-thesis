\section{Notation Policy and Reuse}

Unless explicitly redefined, symbols introduced in this chapter preserve their meanings throughout the thesis.
In particular, $\q,\q'$ denote pre/post states, $\act$ denotes an action,
$\attackvector$ denotes a trace, and $\profit$ denotes objective functions.

This policy reduces ambiguity, supports cross-chapter proof readability,
and enables direct comparison between methods developed for different security layers.
